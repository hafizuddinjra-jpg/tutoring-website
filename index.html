Courses we tutor

From first-year calculus to plant design. Search by course code or topic. Don't see yours? Message us. We can usually find a tutor.

Year 1

  • CPE420 Introduction to chemical engineering

    Units and dimensions, process variables, unit operations and what chemical engineers do.

  • CHE413 Fluid mechanics for chemical engineering

    Fluid statics, Bernoulli, pipe flow and friction losses, pumps.

  • CHE434 Process chemistry

    Reactions, stoichiometry, equilibrium and kinetics used in industrial processes.

  • CPE414 Engineering drawing

    Orthographic and isometric views, sectioning, dimensioning, CAD.

  • MAT435 Calculus for engineers

    Limits, differentiation, integration and their applications.

  • MAT455 Further calculus for engineers

    Partial derivatives, multiple integrals, series, differential equations.

  • CHE430 Organic chemistry

    Functional groups, reaction mechanisms, stereochemistry.

  • CHE433 Thermodynamics

    First and second laws, properties of pure substances, steam tables, power and refrigeration cycles.

  • CHE471 Chemical process principles I

    Material balances on single and multiple units, recycle, bypass and purge.

Year 2

  • CHE531 Chemical process principles II

    Energy balances, enthalpy calculations, reactive systems, humidity and psychrometric charts.

  • CHE463 Heat transfer

    Conduction, convection, radiation, heat exchanger design with LMTD and NTU.

  • CHE545 Mass transfer

    Diffusion, mass transfer coefficients, interphase mass transfer.

  • CHE555 Numerical methods and optimization

    Root finding, linear systems, numerical integration, ODE solvers, optimization.

  • EEE510 Sustainability management

    Sustainable development, environmental management systems, life cycle thinking.

  • CHE502 Reaction engineering I

    Rate laws, mole balances, batch, CSTR and PFR design.

  • CHE522 Transport phenomena

    Shell balances and equations of change for momentum, heat and mass.

  • CHE544 Separation processes

    Distillation with McCabe-Thiele, absorption, stripping, extraction.

  • CHE553 Chemical engineering thermodynamics

    Solution thermodynamics, vapor-liquid equilibrium, chemical reaction equilibrium.

  • CPE501 Chemical process control

    Laplace transforms, transfer functions, PID control, stability.

Year 3

  • CHE505 Reaction engineering II

    Multiple reactions, non-isothermal reactors, catalysis, residence time distribution.

  • CPE520 Project management

    Planning, scheduling with CPM and PERT, cost and risk control.

  • CPE604 Plant design and economics

    Equipment sizing, capital and operating costs, NPV and profitability.

  • CPE614 Process integration

    Pinch analysis, heat exchanger networks, energy targeting.

Final year and electives

  • CPE601 Materials and design of process equipment

    Material selection, corrosion, pressure vessel and equipment design.

  • CPE605 Process simulation

    Building and solving process flowsheets in simulators such as Aspen.

  • CPE606 Process modelling

    First-principles models of process units and how to solve them.

  • CPE615 Process safety

    Hazard identification, HAZOP, risk assessment, fires and explosions, relief systems.

  • CPE622 Process control practices

    Instrumentation, control loop design and controller tuning in real plants.

  • CPE649 Environmental engineering

    Wastewater treatment, air pollution control, solid waste management.

  • CBE658 Food preservation

    Thermal processing, drying, freezing and shelf life.

  • CBE660 Downstream processing

    Cell disruption, centrifugation, filtration, chromatography and crystallization of bioproducts.

  • CPE680 Leadership and professional ethics

    Engineering leadership, codes of ethics, professional responsibility.

  • ENT600 Technology entrepreneurship

    Spotting opportunities, business models, writing a business plan.
